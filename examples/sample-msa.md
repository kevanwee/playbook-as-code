2.1 The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Service during the Term.

4.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to make the Service available 99.0% of the time in each calendar month.
4.2 The Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the service level shall be the Service Credits in Schedule 2.

7.3 The limitation in clause 7.2 shall not apply to any breach of clause 5 (Data Protection) or clause 6 (Intellectual Property).
7.4 The Supplier shall not be liable for any indirect or consequential loss.

8. Term and Termination
8.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months.
8.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice without liability for any remaining Fees.
